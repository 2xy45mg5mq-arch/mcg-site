Kan Vatan: Blood at the Roots

Created and written by Max Coyne-Green

Season One Breakdown  ·  Internal draft, subject to revision

The season

Hatay, Turkey, 2013. A blight is killing the valley's ancient olive trees. Sezen Yılmaz has just killed her father by accident in the family grove, and the only witness is Musa, the Syrian boy who works for her. Her brother Kaan is flying home from New York in disgrace. An American fugitive named Abe is walking toward her farm with Musa's sister. And where her father's blood soaked into the roots, the trees have started to grow.

Ten episodes, late summer to the olive harvest.

What will Sezen give to keep the grove alive, and will the land accept it?

What drives it

Blood feeds the trees. Wherever blood goes into these roots, the dying trees come back. Nobody says it out loud for half the season. Everyone who works it out has to decide what they are willing to do about it.

Three forces close on one farm, each for its own reason.

  • Terrasource. The American agribusiness is running a land grab. It buys the dying valley cheap and sells its cure only to farmers who sign their land over. The Yılmaz grove is the last big holdout, and it sits on the water the whole valley needs.
  • The CIA. Marcy needs Abe erased. He is the witness to a weapons program that armed the people who went on to attack Turkey.
  • Turkish intelligence. Inspector Demir is hunting the men behind the Reyhanlı bombing and believes Abe is one of them.

Terrasource and the CIA are separate interests that hire the same local fixers. Demir is the only one who sees both walk through the same gate.

Three movements

I. ConvergenceEpisodes 1–4The family, Abe and Leyla converge on the grove. It ends with Kaan and Abe bleeding into the roots.

Davut is buried in the grove, and the trees over his grave are the first to recover. Kaan is home, and the deal Sezen signed is the thing neither sibling will name. Terrasource shows its real offer: sign and your trees live under our name, refuse and the water goes.

Abe and Leyla arrive posing as Syrian laborers. Sezen hires them, and Leyla finds her brother Musa alive. Marcy learns what really happened in Syria and concludes that Abe has to disappear. Demir ties the foreigner at the border to the bombing.

The movement ends when Abe breaks cover to defend Leyla. Kaan finds out who he is, and they fight in the grove until both bleed. By morning the trees where they fought are the healthiest on the land, and Kaan has seen it.

II. SiegeEpisodes 5–8The farm is exposed and surrounded. It ends when Demir learns the truth.

Kaan runs to Istanbul and Abe is sent to bring him back. A bar fight there puts Abe's face in front of both Demir and Marcy. Marcy comes to Turkey and hires Terrasource's fixers to run a team the agency can't sanction. Terrasource cuts the water and starts burning trees.

Abe turns the laborers into a defense, and Kaan takes to the violence too easily. Sezen and Abe move toward each other while she watches her brother become someone she doesn't recognize. Rob, the scientist Terrasource fired in the pilot, comes back with what the company's samples showed, and the question opens of whether Terrasource caused the blight.

The movement ends when Demir gets the truth from Leyla and from the paper trail Abe's handler left behind. The man he has been hunting is the one man who can prove Turkey was used. He stops cooperating with Marcy.

III. HarvestEpisodes 9–10The deal, the dawn siege, the earthquake.

Terrasource's ultimatum and Marcy's kill team arrive on the same morning through the same gate. Demir offers Sezen a deal: Abe alive in Turkish custody, the grove left standing, Kaan in the clear.

In the dawn siege Kaan is shot and bleeds into the roots. Sezen takes the deal without telling either man, and Demir leads Abe out of the grove.

Then an earthquake ends the siege, breaks the convoy, and gives the valley back its water. Abe is loose. Kaan lives and knows what his sister did. The trees stand.

Character arcs

What season one leaves lit

Season one ends with Abe a fugitive on the Yılmaz family's own land. The family that took him in is the one that gave him up.

  • Kaan knows. He bled for the grove and learned his sister traded Abe behind his back.
  • Rob has crossed over. Terrasource now has someone who knows the grove from the inside and wants to understand what the trees are doing.
  • The blight. Whether Terrasource caused it may still be unanswered.
  • Marcy has failed. The witness she needed erased is alive and loose.

Still open

  • Where Abe's war crime is fully revealed, and to whom.
  • The steps of Sezen and Abe's relationship between episodes six and nine.
  • How much Sezen learns about Abe from Kaan, and how much from Abe himself.
  • Whether Kaan and Sezen reconcile in the finale or carry the break into season two.
  • Whether the blight question is answered late in season one or held for season two.
  • Where the siege leaves Leyla.
  • What Musa does with what he saw.
