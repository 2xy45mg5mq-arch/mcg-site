LOSING REALITY

A disgraced reality star fights for redemption on a dangerous new competition show produced by a mysterious entity with dramatic plans for his story.

The Story

Ten years after a violent meltdown on a dating show destroyed his life, Rudy is finally a nobody.

But when a viral clip of his worst moment recirculates, he’s outed at work and dragged back into humiliating infamy. Desperate to reclaim his reputation, he agrees to join a new show in which losers and villains from across reality TV compete for the opportunity to finally be a hero. But as the competition heats up at a remote mountain house, Rudy makes a terrifying discovery on set…

…and the real game begins.
