It's not personal.
It's just Manhattan.

When Murry doesn't come home, Sera goes looking. The only lead: the last person he ever mentioned.

The investor in the penthouse.

Life upstairs is everything Murry imagined.

Until he discovers that the man he's pretending to be isn't who he thought.

But there's a catch.

He'll have to make sure everyone sees Nik.

So when Nik has a work emergency out of town, he turns to Murry with an offer: stay with Snacks in the penthouse.

NIK lives at the top.

Unlike the other tenants at the Pyramid, the suave investor in Penthouse B always looks Murry right in the face.

He helps give insulin to his diabetic dog, Snacks. because besides Murry's beard, the two look strikingly similar.

...and there is someone he's always wanted to be.

But Murry's listened to enough hustle culture podcasts to know he can be anyone he wants to be.

Though SERA doesn't look down on her little brother.

She just wants him to stay grounded. Their whole lives, he's been getting stuck in holes, leaving her to dig him out. She just wants him to be happy with who he is.

He's dreaming of the top.

But he lives in his sister's basement.

MURRY opens the door.

That's what he does. Six days a week in the green marble lobby of the storied uptown Pyramid House.

But if you asked him, he wouldn't say he's a doorman. He'd say he's an entrepreneur working as a doorman. Waiting for the right door to open for him.
